# The landing page should make Card Orb's free and European collector focus clearer without becoming a geographic slogan.

## What was said

> en usp's zijn dat het volledig gratis is en europees gefocust, maar dat misschien niet zo benoemen specifiek misschien

> The USPs are that it is completely free and European-focused, but perhaps do not name that too explicitly.

## Context

The public homepage positions Card Orb to prospective collectors.

## Interpretation

The copy should state that the product is free without implying a limited tier and should signal European relevance through familiar pricing and market language rather than a broad regional claim.

## Action

- [x] Refine the free and Cardmarket/euro copy on the landing page.
