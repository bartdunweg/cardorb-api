# The Card Orb homepage does not yet present the product as a mature collection platform.

## What was said

> Kan je de cardorb website meer opzetten als een echt platform landingpage?

> Can you set up the Card Orb website more like a real platform landing page?

## Context

The public homepage at `/` introduces Card Orb to prospective Pokémon-card collectors.

## Interpretation

The homepage should explain the complete personal-collection workflow and provide stronger product proof while remaining accurate about Card Orb's non-social product scope.

## Action

- [x] Replace the sparse landing page with a premium product landing page and a static preview of the real public collection.
