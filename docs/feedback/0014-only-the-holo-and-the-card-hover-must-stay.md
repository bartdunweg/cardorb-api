# The visual identity is not settled, so only the holo effect and the card hover are protected

## What was said

> UI is van mijn betreft nog helemaal niet gedefinieerd, dus ik wil het op zich
> wel helemaal "untitled" hebben.
>
> Daarvoor mag je ook wel weer dingen weggooien of zo. Alleen de hole, het
> hover-effect van een kaart, moet blijven, maar de rest kan je in principe wel
> gewoon, nou, hoeft niet te blijven, snap je wat ik bedoel?

English translation: *"As far as I'm concerned the UI isn't defined at all yet,
so I'd actually like it fully 'Untitled'. You can throw things away again for
that. Only the holo, the hover effect of a card, has to stay — the rest doesn't
in principle have to stay, do you get what I mean?"*

("de hole" is a typo for "de holo" — the holographic card effect in
`app/styles/poke-holo.css`.)

## Context

Said after being told the honest answer to "so no custom CSS any more?": that
2,436 lines of hand-written CSS remain, and that most of it was being kept on
the grounds that it is Card Orb's identity — the glass material, the orb, the
tint blue, holo — per ADR-0056.

So this is a direct correction of that record's premise. ADR-0056 protected four
things as identity because a multiple-choice answer earlier in the same session
had asked to "keep the Card Orb look: glass, orb, holo". That answer was given
about a look nobody had committed to.

## Interpretation

*Hypothesis, not fact.*

The list of protected things drops from four to two, and the two are effects on
a card rather than a design language: the holographic shine
(`app/styles/poke-holo.css`) and the tilt/hover on a card tile (`hover-tilt`,
`Card.tsx`, `CardItem.tsx`). Those are what makes this a Pokémon card app rather
than a generic collection tracker, and they have no Untitled UI equivalent
because they are not components.

Everything else is now up for deletion rather than preservation: the glass
material, `cards.css`'s 1,169 lines, the bespoke token scale, the pill radii.
"Mag je weggooien" is explicit permission, and the reasoning behind it —
"the UI isn't defined yet" — says the cost of losing a considered choice is low
because few of them were considered.

The thing this does **not** obviously settle is the brand: whether "fully
Untitled" means taking their purple, and whether the orb wordmark is UI (goes)
or brand (stays). Those are asked rather than assumed, because ADR-0048 and
ADR-0049 are records about the mark specifically, and a mark generated by a
different repository is not the same kind of thing as a button.

## Action

- [x] Supersede ADR-0056's identity list: holo and the card hover, and nothing
      else, are protected.
- [x] Settle the brand colour and the wordmark before deleting anything, since
      both decide what the token sheet is even for.
- [ ] Then delete rather than convert: `cards.css`, the glass recipe in
      `components.css`, and whatever of `tokens.css` stops having a reader.
- [x] Keep `poke-holo.css` and `hover-tilt` untouched, and say so in the record
      so a later sweep does not take them.
