- Every card and set fact now comes from one rule instead of a rule per field: `src/lib/core/consensus.mjs` declares, per fact, which sources vote and how heavily, and what each of them is known to get wrong, with a reason and the day it was written down. A majority decides; a tie or a split changes nothing and is reported with what each source said; a source with a declared bias stands aside. Two facts were decided by it straight away: 30th Celebration's Classic Collection is named after its parent set, the way Celebrations Classic Collection and Crown Zenith Galarian Gallery always have been, and the five Yellow A Alternate cards TCGplayer sells as holofoil are offered as holos rather than Standard copies.
