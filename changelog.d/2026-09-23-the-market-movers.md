- `GET /v1/catalog/movers` answers the cards whose market price moved most over the last seven days across the whole English catalogue, ten up and ten down, so Home can show a visitor with no account real movers where a signed-in reader sees their own. A card appears once, at the printing that moved most. Ranked by what one copy moved in euros, after the same stray-sale filtering every price line gets, and only among printings at a euro or more at both ends of the week. It answers without a credential, and the same to everybody.
