- A print run (1st Edition or Shadowless) whose TCGplayer market figure is under half its own lowest listing is no longer priced at that figure. Where the listing is at most ten times the figure, the printing is priced at the listing, labelled `lowest-listing` like a printing with no market figure and never summed into a value; where it is more (a placeholder ask), the printing has no price. Every other printing keeps its market figure. A thinly traded run's market figure can be one old sale nothing on offer comes near: Team Rocket's Dark Charizard 1st Edition holo read $121.94 while the cheapest copy was $980, and stood on Home as the week's biggest fall. Judged where the nightly job stores TCGplayer's figures and wherever they are read live. On the price line such a day is held at the last figure the rule believes and marked `held`, as a stray sale is; the history keeps the listing beside the figure for that (`listing_cents`, only on those days). `GET /v1/catalog/movers` leaves out a printing whose figure on the window's last day is held.
